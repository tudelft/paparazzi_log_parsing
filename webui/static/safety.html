Help

Report incident opens the form for a danger, incident or accident; the ? next to Type explains the three. Only the type, date and time and description are required. Administrators get an email with the whole report. Tick "Leave my name out of the notification email" to keep your name out of that email; it is still stored with the report. Emergency: who to call (when there is one) opens the emergency flowchart: who to call after a drone incident, per location and kind of incident. Administrators change the text above the buttons with Edit text (or a click on the text); ⋯ goes back to the default text and uploads, replaces or removes the emergency flowchart (one self-contained HTML page; it stays behind the login, not in the repository).

Report incident

Danger
An occurrence, other than an accident, associated with the operation of an aircraft that affects or could affect the safety of the operation.
Incident
An occurrence, other than an accident, where physical injury, damage to third-party or private property, the safety of people or critical infrastructure was compromised, or unanticipated damage to the aircraft.
Accident
Damage to critical infrastructure, or physical injury requiring medical assistance. Call the emergency services first.

Your name is still stored with the report.

Edit text

The text above Report incident, for everyone: who to contact, where else to report. Plain text.

Emergency flowchart

One self-contained HTML page (its styles and scripts inside, no outside files), at most 2 MiB. Everyone who is signed in sees it behind Emergency: who to call; it runs sandboxed: it cannot reach the logbook or load anything from elsewhere, phone and mail links work. Earlier versions are kept.
