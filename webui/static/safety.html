Danger, incident and accident reporting

Help

Report a danger, incident or accident with the registration form. You see your own reports and their status, and get an email when the status changes; administrators see all reports and who filed them, get an email for each new one and keep the investigation where the organisation stores it (the reference on a report). Tick "Leave my name out of the notification email" to keep your name out of that email; administrators still see it on the report. In Edit mode administrators follow up reports and change the text at the top.
