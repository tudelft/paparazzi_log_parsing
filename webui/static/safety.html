Reporting: report a danger, incident or accident with the registration form, or print a blank copy. You see your own reports and their status; administrators see all reports, get a notice of each new one and keep the investigation where the organisation stores it (the reference on a report). Tick Confidential to leave your name out of the notice. Safety board and Safety promotion are for administrators: the schedule of safety board meetings, the safety promotion programme and the safety meetings held, each with its attendance list. Administrators change the texts and the documents link in Edit mode.
