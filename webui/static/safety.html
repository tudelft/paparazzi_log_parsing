Reporting: report a danger, incident or accident with the registration form, or print a blank copy. You see your own reports and their status, and get an email when the status changes; administrators see all reports and who filed them, get an email for each new one and keep the investigation where the organisation stores it (the reference on a report). Tick "Leave my name out of the notification email" to keep your name out of that email; administrators still see it on the report. Safety board and Safety promotion are for administrators: the schedule of safety board meetings, the safety promotion programme and the safety meetings held, each with its attendance list. In Edit mode administrators follow up reports, add and edit meetings and change the texts and the documents link.
