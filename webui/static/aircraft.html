Aircraft and their aliases

Each aircraft has a unique serial (TUDxxxxx) that stays with it if you rename it. The list can be filled from the organisation CSV (AC Nice Name, Serial Unique, Manager, Current User, Status, Date Bought, Project, Comments). Names in the logs that differ from the nice name are aliases. Removing an aircraft keeps the logs.
