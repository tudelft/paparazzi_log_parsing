Help

Shared documents, for example the ones to show when you are asked for them in the field, and your own licences and qualifications. Pick a group in the tabs, or All for everything you may see. A document opens in a new tab; download saves it. Some documents are only for administrators, for people with a current qualification, or for some profile roles (when person profiles are on): you see a document only when you meet every limit set on it; administrators see all. My qualifications lists your own approved qualifications that are valid today; add or renew them in My account. Administrators set the text at the top, groups and documents in Edit mode; Edit on a document also replaces its file, for example a renewed certificate.
