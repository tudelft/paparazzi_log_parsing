Help

Shared documents, for example the ones to show when you are asked for them in the field, and your own licences and qualifications. Pick a group in the tabs, or All for everything you may see. Search finds documents by title, text or file name. A click on a document opens it in a new tab; the arrow saves it. Hover over a document for its full text and file name; the date is its expiry (orange within 60 days) or, without one, its release date. Some documents are only for administrators, for people with a current qualification of one of some types, or for some profile roles (when person profiles are on): you see a document only when you meet every limit set on it; administrators see all. My qualifications lists your own approved qualifications that are valid today; add or renew them in My account. Administrators see every group (also empty ones) and each document's limits. They add documents with Add document; Manage ▾ adds a group, edits the text at the top (or click the text) and imports. Tick documents for the bar at the bottom: Edit document… (one; it also replaces the file, for example a renewed certificate), Move to group… and Delete…. A group's heading has Edit group and ⋯ (Delete group…). Import ZIP adds or replaces many documents at once from a manifest.csv (template) and the files.
