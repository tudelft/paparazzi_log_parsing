Approval queue

Help

Approve or reject each training pass, qualification or booking (agendas that need approval) here; ▸ opens its evidence and details. Approve shows Undo for a few seconds before it is saved (leaving the page saves it at once). Reject asks for an optional reason, which is mailed to the person. A submission that cannot be approved yet says why; Edit opens it in that person's Qualifications tab. Decided lists what was approved, rejected or withdrawn, also what needed no approval (training passes without approval, imports, bookings by an administrator or approver); click a row for its details.

Qualification types

Help

The kinds of licences and qualifications people can add, in this order. The labels show what someone must fill in when adding one: Evidence (a file), Valid from, Valid until (expiry reminders are sent 30 and 7 days before) and Number (the licence or certificate number, read from a PDF certificate when left empty; Fill numbers from PDFs… does this for approved ones). Internal marks an in-house qualification such as FI or CTR rather than an external licence. The description is shown where people add or see the type. The group puts types under a heading wherever they are listed (adding a qualification, Also covers, Documents, agendas, trainings); types are dragged within their group, and a whole group by the handle on its heading; types without a group come last under Other. Click a type to change it, also its group (an existing group in another case is joined; it then moves to the end of that group); the handle ↕ drags it to another place. An inactive type can no longer be added; existing ones stay. Fill numbers from PDFs…, Check QR codes… and Import qualifications (ZIP)… are under People, Manage ▾. The ZIP import takes a manifest.csv (CSV template) and evidence PDFs to create and approve qualifications in bulk.

Profile options

Help

The choices of the profile everyone fills in after signing in. Supervises offers people with that role as supervisors; Needs supervisor makes naming one required. Max years limits how far ahead the expected end date may be (empty: no limit), and only roles with Indefinite may tick Indefinite instead of a date; profiles outside these limits are asked again at the next sign-in. A department's group is shown next to it; people can also type a department that is not listed. The department list starts empty: add your departments or sections, or Import CSV with one per line as name,group. Drag a row by ↕ to change the order: roles are sorted in this order on this page (Role column and filter, Supervision), for example Staff first. Removing an entry leaves that field empty in the profiles that used it, so those people are asked again. One Save writes both lists and the order.

Supervision

Help

Who supervises whom, from the profiles. Each card lists the people a supervisor supervises, with their role, expected end and projects (those you can open). External supervisors have no account and are grouped by name.

Fill numbers from PDFs

Reads the licence number of approved qualifications whose type requires one and that have none yet, from their PDF certificates; scans are read with OCR (a few seconds each). Existing numbers are never changed.

Check QR codes

Reads the QR code on the evidence of approved and pending licences (PDF, JPEG, PNG; scans too). Most codes are a link to the issuing authority's check of the licence (RDW, DGAC, ENAC, LBA, DAC Luxembourg via Eurocontrol); the UK CAA links to its website only. The authority must match the country of the entered number; a number or date in the code must match the entered one. New uploads are read automatically. Nothing is sent to outside services: the link is shown on the licence for you to open.

Import qualifications

Upload a ZIP that contains manifest.csv and the evidence files. Preview checks people, types, dates and files. Per person and type, the latest period becomes current; older periods go to Archive. Periods already recorded are skipped, so the same ZIP can be imported again. Names not yet on People are added as new users without password or email; unknown types are added as qualification types. A row with only a name adds that person without a qualification.

Import profiles

A CSV with the columns username, role, department, supervisors, end_date, number, github (only username is required; it may also be an email). Only empty fields are filled in: what people already entered stays. Supervisors are separated by semicolons (;). A name or username of someone who can supervise links to them, others become external supervisors. end_date is YYYY-MM-DD or indefinite. Fields may stay empty. Everyone whose profile is filled in this way checks it at their next sign-in. CSV template
