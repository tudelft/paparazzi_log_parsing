Additional flight files

Photos, videos and other recordings

Files are stored once and can be linked to multiple flights, paper records or whole logs. A file linked to a log also appears on its flights. Text and other compressible files use lossless compression; photos, videos and archives stay in their original form when compression would not help. Downloads always restore the exact original bytes.

Sharing: everyone with access to any linked flight/log can download the file. Linking across projects deliberately shares its contents with those projects.
