Categories

Help

Which value a flight gets, the first that applies: set on the flight (Edit flight), indoor, the planned slot (Plan flight in the Agenda), the project, the takeoff location, national rules for flights before 2021, else OPEN. When the project says OPEN and the location something else, the location's applies; an authorisation set on a slot, project or location only counts on the days it is valid. Who may choose: OPEN is for everyone; for the others (and for each authorisation and operator) administrators can list qualification types. Then only holders of a current qualification of one of them choose it, in Plan flight and in Edit flight, and the flight's PIC must hold it too. Nothing listed: everyone. Administrators click a row to change it.

Authorisations

Help

The rules each flight was flown under (its category): national rules (ROC / ROC-light) before EU 2019/947, then OPEN, or SPECIFIC under an operational authorisation. A flight's category is, in this order: set on the flight (Edit flight), indoor, the planned slot, its project, its takeoff location, national rules by date, else OPEN. By date, flights before 31 Dec 2020 are under national rules, and for our own operations a national authorisation listed here that was valid that day gives national rules with it. Set SPECIFIC on the project, the location or the planned slot; an authorisation there only counts on the days it is valid. An exemption (such as a one-off BVLOS exemption) is left out of this date rule: it only applies where it is set. Leave the holder empty for our own authorisations. Who may choose: the qualification types needed to choose it (and those of its category); nothing listed: everyone. The logbook marks SPECIFIC flights without an authorisation, or outside its validity, with (check). Administrators click a row to change or delete it.

Operators

Help

The UAS operator registrations flights are flown under. A flight is under, in this order: the operator set on the flight, that of its planned slot, the operator its PIC flies for, that of its project, that of its takeoff location (before the project's when the project names our own operator), or else our own operator. Nothing has to be filled in per flight: mark our own operator, then choose the operator of external crew below. Anyone can add the operator of a new partner (also from Edit flight and the paper-record form); only administrators change, delete or mark our own (click a row), and list the qualification types needed to choose an operator (Who may choose; nothing listed: everyone). Use the flight filters for own operations only.

External crew

The operator each external person flies for. Their flights count under it unless the flight, project or location says otherwise; (check) in the logbook marks external crew without one. Click a person to set it.
