Help

Complete a questionnaire until you pass. Only the passing answers and the number of attempts are kept. Each submission shows your score and you may retry without limit, so answers can be found by trial and error. A pass writes the linked qualification immediately; administrators do not review it. After a pass expires (or an administrator resets it), you can take the questionnaire again.

Import training

One JSON questionnaire, or a ZIP with that JSON plus JPEG/PNG question images. Preview first; nothing is written until you import. A name that already exists is rejected unless you enable replace.
