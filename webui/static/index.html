Help

Every log you can see: those of your projects and the ones you uploaded. Upload new logs with Upload logs at the top right, or drop files on any page. The list shows 20 logs per page by default, newest first; open a log for its details and flights.

The project is set automatically from the aircraft (then the uploader, GCS operator or PIC). Open a log for its details: Edit log changes its project (admins) or its calendar date when it has none; ⋯ has Re-parse and Delete log. Admins find Re-parse outdated, Re-parse unknown dates and the date suggestions under Manage ▾.

Tags

Tags of the ticked logs and their flights: add one to all of them, or ✕ removes it from all of them. A new name makes a new tag. Flight filters hide logs by their tags, also here.

Additional files

Date suggestions

Tick the dates to use. Each becomes the log's date override with the reason as its note, and can be changed or cleared later on the log. Unticked ones stay suggestions.

Edit log

Deleted records

Logs and paper records an administrator deleted within the retention period, with the reason and a copy of their details. This list cannot be changed.
