Help

Clear matches between a paper record and a log are linked automatically. The cases here need a person: possible duplicate (logs of the same aircraft near the paper takeoff), other aircraft (only another aircraft flew then: a mix-up on paper or in the log, or two aircraft flying) and conflict (a linked paper record names another aircraft, project, place or crew than the log). Every decision is a normal link that can be undone here or in the logbook. Keys: j/k next/previous, 1-9 tick a log, l link ticked, n not the same, s skip.
