Help

Pick an item in the tabs, or All for every item you may see. Click a booking to open it; drag in an empty part of a day to book that time (a click books an hour). Drag a booking you may change to another time or day: it opens with the new times, and Save moves it. Options a booking ticks show as tags, with their colour; the block's colour is the item's. With one item there are no tabs. Subscribe gives private links for your calendar app, for all items or one. Bookings of the same item overlap only when both set a shared option (for example "Others allowed") and neither sets a blocking one (for example "Demo"); an item without options never overlaps. Some items need a current qualification to book, or to see their agenda at all. Some need approval: your booking then shows as Pending (striped) and holds its time until an administrator or approver approves or rejects it; moving it asks again. You can change or delete your own bookings; administrators can book for someone else and change any booking. Administrators also see items that are closed for bookings, add items with Manage ▾ → Add bookable item…, change the open item with Edit item (⋯ deletes it) and drag the tabs to order them. Times are local.

Subscribe in your calendar

Add a link to Outlook, Apple Calendar, Thunderbird or another calendar app ("subscribe" or "add calendar from URL"). The app checks it for changes every so often, and shows the bookings you may see here. The link is private to you: anyone with it can read these bookings, so replace it if it leaks. The app can only reach it while it can reach this site (for example with the VPN on); web calendars such as Google Calendar run on their own servers and may not reach it.
