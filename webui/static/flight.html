Wind and weather

Historical weather is an hourly model estimate near takeoff, not an onboard measurement. Fetching sends the takeoff location (rounded to 0.01°) and date to Open-Meteo. Wind is at 10 m above ground; direction is where it comes from.

Historical data: Open-Meteo / ECMWF, CC BY 4.0. Free API for non-commercial use only.
